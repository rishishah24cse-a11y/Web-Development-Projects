Our Story

Where Cuisine Becomes
an Experience.

Boland's Multicuisine is built on the belief that a meal is more than what arrives on the plate. Each dish begins with ingredients sourced at their seasonal peak, shaped by modern technique, and finished with a presentation that rewards attention.

Our dining room is designed with the same care as our menu — warm materials, considered lighting, and service that anticipates rather than interrupts. Every visit is composed, not repeated.

— The Boland's Multicuisine Culinary Team

Crafted With Intention.

Chef Adrian Laurent

Executive Chef

Adrian's cuisine is rooted in restraint — letting a single seasonal ingredient lead, and using technique only to reveal it more clearly. His menus change with the market, not the calendar.

Trained across kitchens in Lyon and Tokyo before returning home to open Boland's Multicuisine, he brings a contemporary, precise sensibility to dishes that still feel personal.
